# Analysis Plan

1. Establish a reporting-safe encounter denominator using completeness and validity flags.
2. Calculate site-level data-quality exception rate, 30-day readmission rate, and average length of stay.
3. Join quality-event and refresh-run evidence to distinguish source remediation from reporting-operation issues.
4. Track self-service adoption after demonstrations, so leadership reports are not only delivered but used.
5. Prioritize remediation huddles with the highest exception-rate sites; reassess after two reporting cycles.

EDA coverage: row-count checks, missingness flags, service-line mix, site-level outlier ranking, refresh failure monitoring, and month-over-month adoption trend.
