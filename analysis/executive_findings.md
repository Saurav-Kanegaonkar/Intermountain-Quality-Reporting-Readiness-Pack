# Executive Findings

## What the evidence says

The generated analysis contains 100k+ synthetic encounter rows across 20 fictional sites, plus reporting refresh, quality-event, and adoption records. The site scorecard prioritizes a small set of elevated exception-rate sites before leadership reports are treated as decision-ready. The adoption view makes customer enablement observable rather than assumed.

## Recommendations

1. Require a pre-distribution SQL validation gate for completeness, validity, and duplicate-key exceptions; escalate any site above the 2.5% exception threshold to a data-owner huddle.
2. Pair weekly quality-and-utilization reviews with a visible refresh-status panel, so a late or failed source does not silently reach regional leadership.
3. Run short demonstrations for the least-active report audiences, then measure monthly active-user rate and self-service sessions rather than counting report deliveries.

## Success measures after action

Track exception rate below 2.5%, failed refreshes per reporting cycle, time-to-resolution for reporting holds, and active-user rate by report audience. These are demonstration metrics from synthetic data, not observed Intermountain results.
